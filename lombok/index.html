A gentle 3-day rhythm

Assumes you stay around Kuta Lombok and rent a car, not a packed tour schedule.

DAY 01

Arrive & settle in

Airport → hotel → pool → explore Kuta town → ayam taliwang dinner. Keep this day flexible with flight timing.

DAY 02

Beach & headland

Early Tanjung Aan → lunch → hotel rest → Bukit Merese if dry; or swap to Selong Belanak for a whole beach day.

DAY 03

Culture before flying

Breakfast → Sade village or a short local market stop → airport. Check your return flight before committing.
